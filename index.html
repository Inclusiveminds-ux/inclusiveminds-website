About NeuroKite

I started NeuroKite to give children with special educational needs the kind of individual attention that's hard to find in a special needs classroom of four to eight, or a mainstream classroom of thirty — sessions paced to how each child actually learns, while still supporting what they're covering at school for better mastery of the syllabus.

I work with children from as early as age 4, across homeschooling families, SEN schools, and mainstream schools across Johannesburg. I'm an inclusive educator, trained across Autism Spectrum Disorder, learning disabilities, hearing impairment, and braille literacy.

  • Degree in Special Needs Education
  • 6 years' experience working with children with additional needs
  • Trained across ASD, learning disabilities, hearing impairment, and braille literacy
  • Police clearance on file, available on request

What sessions look like

Flexible formats to fit how — and where — your child learns best.

01 Homework & academic support

After-school help with homework, assignments, and exam preparation, aligned with what your child is covering at school. Progress notes shared with parents after each session.

02 Homeschool tutoring

Structured, CAPS-aligned lessons for homeschooling families, with an individualised learning plan built around your child's pace and goals.

03 Parent consultations

A dedicated session to talk through progress, set goals, and share strategies you can use with your child at home, between lessons.
